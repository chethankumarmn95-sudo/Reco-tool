01 — Our Solutions

One ledger for every part of the money trail.

01 E-commerce Reconciliation Orders from Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart and marketplace channels matched line-by-line against what was actually delivered and paid.
02 Payment & Bank Reconciliation Gateway settlements, UTRs and bank credits linked back to the order that generated them, automatically.
03 Settlement Reconciliation Marketplace and COD settlement reports checked against dispatch and delivery data to surface true payouts.
04 Order & Delivery Reconciliation Delivery-partner status — delivered, RTO, lost, cancelled — reconciled against order and payment state.
05 Receivables Tracking Pending amounts and ageing receivables tracked per order, per channel, until they’re actually collected.
06 MIS & Financial Analytics Board-ready summaries — gross value, net settlement, deduction breakdowns — generated straight from reconciled data.
07 Exception & Pending Reports Every order that doesn’t tie out gets flagged with a reason, so nothing is written off without being looked at.

06 — Security

The public site and the ledger are two different rooms.

No visitor sees a single figure until they’ve signed in. What’s reconciled stays behind the door.

Secure authentication

Every session starts with a verified sign-in, no exceptions.

Authorised access only

Accounts are issued individually; there is no public sign-up.

Data privacy

Reconciliation data is never surfaced on the public site, in any form.

Role-based access

Each account sees only the tools and data it’s permitted to.
